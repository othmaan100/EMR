# Inpatients and wards

**Menu:** Clinical → Inpatients / Wards · **Used by:** doctors, nurses, midwives; cashiers view

## The bed board

**Inpatients / Wards** shows each ward with its beds: occupied (with the patient), available, being cleaned or out of service. Select an occupied bed to open the patient's inpatient chart.

## Admitting a patient

1. From the patient folder (or a consultation) select **Admit**.
2. Choose the ward and an available bed, the admitting doctor and the reason for admission.
3. Save. An admission number (ADM…) is issued and a separate admission bill is opened. Consider taking a **deposit** at the cashier.

## The inpatient chart

- **Ward notes:** doctors' and nurses' progress notes, dated and signed (**Add note**).
- **Vital signs:** **Record vitals** with early-warning score and trend charts.
- **Drug chart:** prescribe ward medication with **Chart & send** — it appears on the drug chart and goes to the pharmacy. Nurses select **Record dose** at each administration and mark it **Given**, **Refused** or **Held / omitted**; the last dose given is shown. **Stop** ends a medication.
- **Lab and imaging:** order tests from the ward with **Request**; results appear on the chart when released.
- **Book surgery** opens a theatre booking for the patient.

## Transfer

Select **Transfer** to move the patient to another bed or ward. The bed history is kept, and bed charges follow the new ward's rate from the next day.

## Daily bed charges

Each ward can have a daily accommodation charge (set on the ward). Charges are posted automatically every night (just after midnight) for every admitted patient. **Update bed charges** posts any missing days immediately — useful before final billing.

## Discharge

1. Select **Discharge** and complete the **final diagnosis**, **discharge summary**, discharge medications, follow-up instructions and the discharge type (home, referred, against medical advice, died, absconded).
2. Save. The bed is released (marked for cleaning) and the admission is closed.
3. Print the **Discharge summary** for the patient and referral letter purposes.

Admission bills can only be claimed from an HMO after discharge.
