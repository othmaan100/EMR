# Maternity and child health

**Menus:** Clinical → Maternity, Clinical → Immunization · **Used by:** midwives, doctors, nurses

## Antenatal booking

1. From a female patient's folder select **Book ANC**.
2. Enter the last menstrual period (LMP) — the expected date of delivery (EDD) and gestational age are calculated — or an EDD from an ultrasound scan.
3. Record the obstetric history (gravida, parity, abortions, living children) and any risk factors (e.g. previous caesarean section, age under 18 or over 35, grand multiparity).
4. Save. Pregnancies with risk factors are marked **High risk** and highlighted.

## Antenatal visits

At each visit record gestation, blood pressure, weight, fundal height, presentation, fetal heart rate, urine protein, oedema, haemoglobin and interventions (e.g. IPTp, tetanus-diphtheria (Td), iron/folic acid, insecticide-treated net). Dangerous values — high blood pressure, proteinuria, abnormal fetal heart rate, low haemoglobin — raise **alerts** on the visit.

## Labour and the partograph

1. Select **Start labour / partograph** on the pregnancy.
2. Record partograph entries at intervals: cervical dilatation, descent, contractions, fetal heart rate, liquor, moulding, maternal pulse, BP and temperature.
3. The **partograph** chart plots dilatation against the alert and action lines; values outside safe limits are flagged.

## Delivery and babies

Select **Record delivery**: date and time, mode (spontaneous vaginal, assisted vaginal, vaginal breech, elective or emergency caesarean section), perineum, blood loss, complications and outcome for the mother. For each baby record sex, weight, Apgar scores and outcome. **Babies born alive are registered automatically** as patients (with their own hospital numbers) and linked to the mother; their immunization schedule starts from the date of birth.

Delivery charges (vaginal or caesarean) are added if priced. A caesarean section can be booked in theatre directly from the pregnancy (**Book caesarean section**).

## Postnatal care

Record postnatal visits for the mother (and baby): blood pressure, uterus, lochia, breastfeeding, family planning advice. Select **Close pregnancy record** for losses or transfers out.

## Immunization

The **Immunization** page lists children due or **overdue** (more than 28 days past due). For each child:

- The **schedule** shows every vaccine in the national schedule (BCG, Hepatitis B, OPV, Pentavalent, PCV, Rotavirus, IPV, Measles-rubella, Yellow fever) with its due date and status: given, due, overdue, upcoming.
- Record a dose with date, batch number and site.
- Print the **immunization card**.

If SMS is set up, carers receive a reminder three days before each dose is due.
