# Staff, departments, roles and permissions

## Staff accounts

**Menu:** Administration → Staff

- **Add staff:** name, username, email, phone, staff ID, designation, department, role(s) and a temporary password. The person must change it at first sign-in.
- **Edit** details and roles. A staff member can hold several roles (e.g. Doctor and Anaesthetist).
- **Deactivate** an account when someone leaves — they can no longer sign in, but everything they recorded stays attributed to them. Accounts are never deleted.
- **Reset password** sets a new temporary password (to be changed at next sign-in).
- **Unlock** an account locked after too many wrong passwords.

The staff list can be filtered by role, department and status. The last active Super Admin cannot be deactivated or lose the role.

Many staff at once? Use **Data Import → Staff accounts**.

## Departments

**Menu:** Administration → Departments. Departments (clinical, nursing, diagnostic, pharmacy, administrative, finance, support) group staff, clinics and wards, and are used for store requisitions and consumption reports.

## Roles and permissions

**Menu:** Administration → Roles & Permissions. The permission matrix shows every role against every permission, grouped by area. Tick or untick permissions to change a role, or **create a new role** (e.g. "HMO Desk Officer") with exactly the permissions needed. Changes take effect at the user's next page load and are recorded in the audit log.

> Give each person the least access they need. Review roles when someone changes job.
