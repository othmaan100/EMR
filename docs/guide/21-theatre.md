# Theatre and surgery

**Menu:** Clinical → Theatre · **Used by:** surgeons, anaesthetists, theatre nurses

## Booking an operation

1. From the patient folder, ward chart or pregnancy select **Book surgery**.
2. Choose the procedure, theatre, surgeon, anaesthetist, date and time, expected duration, urgency (elective, urgent, emergency) and anaesthesia type.
3. Save (number SUR…). The system warns if the theatre, surgeon or anaesthetist is already booked at that time.

The **Theatre** list shows today's and upcoming operations by theatre. Operations can be rescheduled, postponed or cancelled with a reason.

## Before surgery

- **Pre-operative assessment:** ASA grade, fasting, consent, allergies, investigations, site marking and notes.
- **WHO Surgical Safety Checklist** in three phases, which must be completed in order and in full:
  1. **Sign in** — before induction of anaesthesia (identity, site, consent, pulse oximeter, allergies, airway and blood-loss risk).
  2. **Time out** — before skin incision (team introduced, patient/site/procedure confirmed, antibiotics, imaging displayed).
  3. **Sign out** — before the patient leaves theatre (procedure recorded, counts correct, specimens labelled, recovery concerns).

## During surgery

The anaesthetist records the **anaesthesia record**: technique and timings, and observations (pulse, blood pressure, SpO₂, drugs and fluids) during the operation.

## Operation note

The surgeon writes the operation note — procedure performed, findings, complications, blood loss, specimens, post-operative instructions — and completes the operation. The procedure and the theatre fee are charged (if priced). The operation note can be printed.

## Report

**Theatre activity** (Reports) shows operations by procedure, emergencies, cancellations, average duration and surgeon workload.
