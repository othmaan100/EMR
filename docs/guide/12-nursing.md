# Nursing and triage

**Menu:** Clinical → Nursing / Triage · **Used by:** nurses, midwives, doctors

## Triage

1. Open **Nursing / Triage** — the worklist shows patients waiting for triage.
2. Select the patient and record the **vital signs**: blood pressure, temperature, pulse, respiratory rate, oxygen saturation (and whether on oxygen), level of consciousness, weight and height (BMI is calculated), pain score and blood glucose.
3. Set the visit priority and add a triage note if needed.
4. Save. The patient moves to **Waiting for doctor**.

## Early-warning score (NEWS2)

For adults, the system calculates the National Early Warning Score from the vital signs and shows the risk:

| Score | Risk | Response |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 | Low | Continue routine monitoring |
| Any single value scoring 3 | Low–medium | Urgent ward-based review |
| 5–6 | Medium | Urgent review by a clinician |
| 7 or more | High | Emergency assessment by a clinical team; consider critical care |

Abnormal values are highlighted in colour. The score supports — it never replaces — clinical judgement.

## Vital signs history and charts

The patient's **Vitals** page lists all readings and draws trend charts (blood pressure, pulse, temperature, SpO₂, weight) once there are at least two readings. A reading entered in error can be **voided** with a reason; it stays visible, struck through.

## Nursing notes

Add dated nursing notes from the patient folder or the ward. Notes cannot be edited later; add a new note to correct or update.
