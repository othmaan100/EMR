# Patient portal

The portal lets patients see their own records on a phone or computer. It is switched on in Hospital Settings → Preferences. Patients sign in at the portal address (the hospital's EMR address followed by **/portal**).

## Giving a patient access (staff)

1. Open the patient's folder. In the **Patient portal** card select **Give portal access** (permission: records officers and administrators).
2. A one-page **access letter** is shown with the patient's hospital number and a one-time **activation code** (e.g. K7PM-3QXH), valid for 72 hours. Print it or read it to the patient. Optionally send the code by SMS.
3. The code is shown only once. If it is lost or the patient forgets their password, select **New activation code** — the old password stops working.
4. **Switch off access** at any time.

## What patients do

1. Go to the portal, select **Activate your account**, enter the hospital number and activation code, and choose a password (at least 8 characters, letters and numbers).
2. After that, sign in with the hospital number and password.

In the portal patients can:

- see upcoming appointments, **request an appointment** (clinic, preferred date, morning or afternoon, reason) and cancel appointments;
- see **lab results and imaging reports once released** by the hospital, and print them;
- see bills, the amount owed and receipts — and **pay online** if the hospital offers it;
- see the **immunization card**;
- check their details and change their password.

A mother can switch to her children's records (babies registered at birth under her).

## Safety

- Patients only ever see their own (and their children's) records.
- Accounts lock after 5 wrong passwords; patients are signed out after 15 minutes without activity.
- Sign-ins and every result viewed are recorded in the audit log.
- The portal is not for emergencies; it says so on every page.
