# Pharmacy and drug inventory

**Menus:** Clinical → Pharmacy, Clinical → Drug Inventory · **Used by:** pharmacists; store/procurement staff for ordering

## Dispensing a prescription

1. Open **Pharmacy**. Tabs: **To dispense**, **Partially dispensed**, **Dispensed today**.
2. Select the prescription. Each line shows the directions, quantity prescribed and given so far, and usable stock.
3. Enter the quantity to give now, or mark a line **not available** with a reason (e.g. "Out of stock – patient to buy outside").
4. If there is an **allergy alert** you must tick that you have checked with the prescriber or patient.
5. Select **Dispense**. Stock is taken from the batch closest to expiry first; expired batches are never used. The drug is charged per unit to the patient's bill (if priced).

A prescription can be dispensed in parts; it stays under "Partially dispensed" until complete.

## Pay-first (optional)

If the hospital has switched on **pharmacy pay-first** (Hospital Settings → Preferences):

1. Enter the quantities and select **Price & send to cashier** — the items go on the bill; stock is not touched yet.
2. The patient pays at the cashier.
3. Back in the pharmacy, paid lines show "paid — ready to dispense"; select **Dispense paid items**.

Insurance-covered shares never block dispensing. Marking a priced but unpaid line "not available" cancels its charge.

## Drug inventory

**Drug Inventory** lists every drug with stock on hand, reorder level and batches. Filters show items **at or below reorder level** and **batches expiring soon**.

- **Receive stock:** enter the supplier, invoice number and date, then one line per batch (drug, batch number, expiry date, quantity, unit cost). A goods-received note (GRN) number is issued. Deliveries against a purchase order are received on the order instead (see Stores & procurement).
- **Adjust a batch:** for damage, expiry write-off or stock-count differences, adjust the batch with a reason. Every movement is kept in the drug's ledger.
- **Suppliers** are managed from the inventory page.
