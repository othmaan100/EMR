# Patient registration and records

**Menu:** Clinical → Patients · **Used by:** records officers, nurses, cashiers (view), all clinicians (view)

## Registering a new patient

1. Go to **Patients → Register patient**.
2. Enter the patient's details. Required fields are marked with a red star: first name, surname and sex. Give the **date of birth**; if it is not known, enter the **age in years** instead and the system records an estimated date of birth.
3. Add a phone number (needed for SMS reminders), address and **next of kin**.
4. Choose the **payment type**: Self-pay (cash), Health insurance / HMO, Corporate / company, Staff or Free / waiver. For insurance and corporate patients select the payer and enter the enrollee (member) number and expiry date.
5. Record **allergies** (e.g. "Penicillin — rash"). They appear on every clinical screen and are checked when drugs are prescribed and dispensed.
6. Optionally take a **photo** with the webcam or upload one.
7. Select **Register patient**. A hospital number is issued (e.g. PT-000124) and the registration fee is charged if one is priced.

**Possible duplicates:** if a patient with the same name and date of birth, or the same phone number, already exists, the system lists them before saving. Open the existing record instead of creating a second one. Only tick **This is a different person — register anyway** if you are sure.

**National ID (NIN) verification:** if the hospital has set up NIN verification, a **Verify** button appears next to the NIN field. With the patient's consent, enter the 11-digit NIN and select Verify: empty fields (names, date of birth, sex, phone) are filled in, and any differences from what you typed are shown. A "Verified" badge is then shown on the patient's record.

## The patient folder

The folder is the single view of a patient: demographics, allergies, current visit or admission, vital signs, lab and imaging results, confirmed diagnoses (problem list), prescriptions, operations, pregnancies, immunization status, account balance and portal access. Buttons at the top start common actions — **Check in**, **Book** appointment, **Book surgery**, **Book ANC**, **Admit**, **Print card**, **Edit**.

Every opening of a patient folder is recorded in the audit log.

## Hospital card

Select **Print card** to print a card with the hospital number, name and barcode. Patients should bring it to every visit.

## Editing and archiving

- **Edit** changes demographic and contact details. Changes are recorded in the audit log.
- **Archive** (permission "Archive patient records") hides a record created by mistake from searches. Archived records are kept, not deleted.
- To record a death, edit the record and enter the date of death; the patient is then marked deceased and cannot be checked in or booked.

## Mothers and babies

Babies born in the hospital are registered automatically from the delivery record and linked to their mother. The link appears on both folders and lets the mother see her baby's immunization card in the patient portal.
