# Consultation

**Menu:** Clinical → Clinic Queue → patient · **Used by:** doctors (and other clinicians with consultation permission)

## Starting a consultation

From the queue select the patient and **Start consultation**. The consultation page shows the patient banner (with allergies), the latest vital signs and early-warning score, previous visits, results and current medicines.

## Writing the note

The note has sections: presenting complaint, history, past medical and surgical history, drug history, family and social history, review of systems, examination, assessment and plan. Select **Save notes** regularly — the note is not saved automatically. Only you can edit it, and only until it is signed.

## Diagnoses

Search the **ICD-10** list by code or words (e.g. "malaria", "B54") and add diagnoses as provisional or confirmed; mark one as primary. Confirmed diagnoses build the patient's problem list and are used on HMO claim forms and in the Top Diagnoses report.

## Orders

- **Laboratory:** select one or more tests and a priority (routine or urgent). The request goes to the lab worklist.
- **Imaging:** choose the examination, priority and clinical indication (required, for the radiologist).
- **Prescription:** choose the drug from the formulary (or type a non-formulary drug), dose, route, frequency, duration and quantity. If the patient is allergic to the drug, a warning appears; you must confirm to override, and the override is recorded.

Orders are charged to the patient's bill when priced. An order can be **cancelled** until it has been processed; its charge is then voided automatically.

## Specialty clinics

In a dental, eye or physiotherapy clinic a button at the top opens the dental chart, eye examination or physiotherapy record (see the chapter on specialty clinics).

## Signing and follow-up

When finished, select **Sign**. Optionally book a **follow-up** appointment in the same step. Signing locks the note and completes the visit.

**Addenda:** to add information after signing (e.g. a late result), add an **addendum**. Addenda are dated, show the author, and cannot be edited.

## Printing a prescription

Select **Print** on the prescription to give the patient a copy (e.g. when a drug is not stocked).
