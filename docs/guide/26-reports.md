# Reports and analytics

**Menu:** Finance & Reports → Reports · **Permissions:** operational, clinical, financial and staff reports are separate permissions.

The Reports page shows headline figures for the chosen period (new patients, visits, admissions, money collected) and the list of reports you may open. In each report:

- choose the **date range** (and, where offered, a clinic or ward);
- read the **summary tiles**, the **chart** and the **table** below it (the table always contains the same figures as the chart);
- **Export** to CSV / Excel or **Print**.
