# Specialty clinics: dental, eye and physiotherapy

**Where:** patient folder → **Specialty clinics** card, or the button at the top of a consultation in a dental, eye or physiotherapy clinic · **Used by:** doctors, dental therapists, optometrists, physiotherapists

A clinic's type (General, Dental, Eye / optometry, Physiotherapy) is set under Administration → Clinics.

## Dental chart

- The **tooth chart** uses FDI numbering, drawn as you face the patient. Each tooth is coloured by its latest condition (caries, filled, missing, extracted, crown, root canal treated, fractured, abscess…); a yellow dot means treatment is planned. Children under 13 also see their primary (milk) teeth.
- Click a tooth (or choose "Whole mouth"), tick surfaces (M, O, D, B, L) and choose the type:
  - **Finding** — what you see (condition required).
  - **Treatment plan** — treatment to do later (treatment required).
  - **Treatment done today** — charged immediately.
- For treatment, set the condition the tooth will have afterwards (e.g. Filled, Extracted) so the chart updates when it is done.
- Planned treatment is charged only when you select **Done**; it can also be cancelled.

## Eye examination

Record for each eye: visual acuity unaided and with pinhole/glasses (Snellen, 6/5 to NPL), eye pressure (IOP), refraction (sphere, cylinder, axis, near add), anterior segment and fundus findings, pupillary distance, diagnosis and plan. Tick **Prescribe spectacles** to print a **spectacle prescription**, and optionally charge the refraction fee.

**Alerts:** eye pressure above 21 mmHg, or best vision of 6/60 or worse, is highlighted in red and recorded.

## Physiotherapy

1. **New assessment:** body region, complaint, objective findings (range of motion, strength, special tests), pain score 0–10, goals, plan and number of sessions planned. The assessment fee is charged if priced.
2. **Record a session** each time: treatments given (exercise therapy, manual therapy, TENS, ultrasound, heat, ice, traction, gait training…), pain before and after, notes. Each session fee is charged if priced.
3. A chart shows pain before and after each session.
4. **Discharge** with an outcome (goals met, improved, no change, worse, stopped attending, referred on) and home advice.

## Report

**Specialty clinic activity** shows dental treatments by type, eye examinations, spectacles prescribed, raised eye pressures, physiotherapy assessments, sessions, outcomes and average pain reduction.
