# Getting started

## Signing in

1. Open the EMR address in a web browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox). Your IT officer will give you the address.
2. Enter your **username** (or email) and **password**, then select **Sign in**.
3. The first time you sign in, and after an administrator resets your password, you must choose a new password.

**Password rules:** at least 8 characters, with both letters and numbers. Never share your password — everything you do is recorded under your name.

**Locked out?** After 5 wrong passwords in a row the account is locked for 15 minutes. An administrator can unlock it earlier from your staff profile.

**Automatic sign-out:** if the computer is left idle (30 minutes by default) you are signed out to protect patient data. A warning appears one minute before; select **Stay signed in** to continue.

**Security notice:** after signing in, the dashboard shows when and from where you last signed in. If it wasn't you, tell the administrator immediately and change your password (menu → **My profile**).

## The screen layout

- **Sidebar (left):** the modules you are allowed to use, grouped under Clinical, Stores & Procurement, Finance & Reports and Administration.
- **Top bar:** patient search, your name, **My profile** (change password, update your phone) and **Sign out**.
- **Dashboard:** "Waiting for you" tiles show your work queues — for example patients awaiting triage, lab samples to collect, prescriptions to dispense, claims to batch or requisitions to issue. Select a tile to open that list.

## Finding a patient

Type a hospital number, name, phone number or old folder number into the search box and select the patient. The **patient folder** opens, showing demographics, allergies, visits, results, admissions, prescriptions, bills and quick action buttons.

> Allergies are shown in a yellow banner on every clinical screen. Always check it before prescribing or dispensing.

## Printing

Hospital cards, lab labels and reports, imaging reports, prescriptions, invoices, receipts, claim forms, purchase orders and spectacle prescriptions open in a print view with the hospital letterhead. Use the **Print** button (or Ctrl + P). Choose "Save as PDF" in the print dialog to send a document electronically.

## Good practice

- Sign out when you leave a shared computer.
- Record information at the time you do the work — times are captured automatically.
- If something looks wrong (wrong patient, wrong charge), do not work around it: correct it through the proper action (void, cancel, addendum) so the audit trail stays complete.
